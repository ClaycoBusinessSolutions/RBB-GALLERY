# RBB — museum presentation review

The presentation is organized as a visit: encounter the collection, pause on a statement, enter the proposed spaces, then connect them to the company's shared history. The permanent RBB gallery leads; the exhibition content remains adaptable.

## What changed

- **Collection opening:** a slowly moving field draws from 249 catalogue entries, with only 24 artwork elements active at once. Art keeps its original proportions and can be opened with attribution. The field recedes as the first statement takes over.
- **Slide rhythm:** full-screen slides alternate generous typography, simple spatial studies and inhabited concept films. A presenter controls the pace with arrows or the chapter menu. Films play once and hold, with Replay available.
- **Arc transition:** adapted from the licensed [Osmo Arc Scroll Transition](https://www.osmo.supply/resource/arc-scroll-transition). The curved edge belongs to the incoming slide; the outgoing image remains present until the reveal completes. The model's first frame is prepared before it becomes visible.
- **G menu:** adapted from the exact proportions and motion of [Osmo Footer Parallax Effect](https://www.osmo.supply/resource/footer-parallax-effect). The presentation lifts to reveal three typographic columns and a large RBB GALLERY wordmark below. Repeated G presses reverse the animation; Escape restores the slide.
- **Supporting text:** a restrained adaptation of [Queued Scroll Reveal](https://www.osmo.supply/resource/queued-scroll-reveal) gives captions a short stagger. Hero statements and artwork are kept clear of extra effects. This source was inspected through the signed-in Vault alongside the arc and footer code.
- **Spaces and lighting:** one lightweight Three.js canvas is shared by the model slides. Concept options, Spatial/Plan and Day/Night preserve the selected space. The timeline column is restored, with its exact position still requiring design-team verification.
- **Films:** four new Kling 3.0 scenes show independent visitor movement around the model table, glass floor, shelves and timeline. Each uses a reimagined photographic keyframe based on the corresponding PowerPoint rendering. Original renderings remain accessible for comparison.

## Visual direction

The quiet grid, warm paper, restrained labels, careful artwork spacing and selective red draw on the museum precedents documented in [MUSEUM_DIRECTION.md](build/research/MUSEUM_DIRECTION.md). David Chipperfield and Kiku Obata are references for the design approach, not collaborators on this presentation. The earlier Davison Ancheril, BIG and Muse reference captures remain in `../RBB_Experience_Light/build/reference-study/`; the current version translates that research into a slide sequence.
